1. Overview

Screen Tax is a financial accountability tool that tracks the time you spend on selected apps and automatically charges your credit card on a weekly basis. Charges are aggregated and donated monthly to a cause you select during onboarding. The App is designed to create financial incentives for reducing screen time.

3. How Charges Work

Screen Tax tracks foreground usage of apps you select and applies a per-hour rate that you configure. Each app may have a free daily allowance (in minutes) before charges begin. The charge formula is:

  • Billable minutes = Total usage minutes − Free allowance minutes (minimum 0)
  • Charge = (Billable minutes ÷ 60) × Hourly rate

Weekly charges are processed automatically every Saturday at 9:00 PM local time. Charges below Stripe's minimum transaction amount of $0.50 are skipped for that week.

Important — Uninstall Policy: Screen Tax protects against charge avoidance by periodically committing your accrued weekly total to our backend. If you uninstall the App before Saturday's charge is processed, the most recently committed weekly total will still be charged to your card. By using Screen Tax, you explicitly acknowledge and consent to this policy.
