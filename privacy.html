Screen Tax

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Screen Tax ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. We built Screen Tax with a minimal-data philosophy — your usage data stays on your device.

The short version: We don't run accounts or logins. Your screen time data never leaves your phone. The only data that goes to a server is what's necessary to process your payment.

1. Information We Collect

Data Type What It Is Where It's Stored
App Usage Data Daily foreground minutes per app, collected via Android's Usage Access permission On your device only (local Room database)
App Settings Which apps you track, free allowance minutes, hourly rates On your device only
Payment Identifiers Stripe Customer ID, last four digits of your card, card brand (e.g. Visa) On your device only (DataStore)
Weekly Charge Total Your accrued weekly charge amount, sent to our backend periodically Our backend server (Railway) — used to process Saturday charges and protect against uninstall avoidance
Donation Preference Your selected donation destination (charity, PETA, or NRA Foundation) On your device and sent to our backend at charge time
Payment History Charge timestamps, amounts, and success/failure status On your device only (local Room database)

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account credentials.
  • We do not store your full card number, CVV, or billing address — this is handled entirely by Stripe.
  • We do not transmit your detailed app usage history to any server.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data to any third party.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Processing weekly charges: Your accrued weekly total is sent to our backend to charge your card on Saturday evenings and to protect against charge avoidance if the app is uninstalled.
  • Donations: Your donation preference is used to direct monthly aggregate donations to your chosen organization.
  • App functionality: Usage data is used solely within the app to calculate charges and display your dashboard.

4. Third-Party Services

Stripe — We use Stripe to process all payments. When you set up a card, Stripe collects and stores your payment information directly. We receive only a Customer ID and card display info (last 4 digits, brand) in return. Stripe's Privacy Policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

We do not use any other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Data Retention

  • App usage records are stored locally and can be deleted by the user from within the app's Settings screen.
  • Weekly charge totals stored on our backend are overwritten on each sync and are not retained after the charge is processed.
  • If you uninstall the app, local data is deleted automatically by Android. Any pending charge that was committed to our backend will be processed on the next Saturday and then removed.

6. Children's Privacy

Screen Tax is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor has used the App, please contact us at jreeher@outlook.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the limited data that transits our backend, including HTTPS encryption for all API communication. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

Since we hold minimal personal data, your primary controls are within the app itself:

  • Delete specific days of usage data from Settings → Data.
  • Update or remove your payment card at any time.
  • Contact us to request deletion of any backend-stored charge data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated within the App. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions or concerns? Reach us at jreeher@outlook.com.
